# Our Right to Build (ORB)

> **Status: proposed initiative / independent research and concept development.** ORB is not yet an incorporated entity, a 501(c)(3), a fiscal-sponsored program, a government partner, or an approved development project. It has no land control, committed funding, or authority to certify firms. Site ideas and visualizations are proposals, not permitted projects or engineering designs.

**Our land. Our communities. Our right to build.**

Our Right to Build (ORB) is a proposed Black-American reparative-development initiative focused on one practical question: **Do communities, firms, regions, and public agencies have the capacity to build what policy assumes they can build?** ORB proposes to identify readiness gaps, connect capacity to demand, and measure whether resources lead to durable assets and opportunities.

## Three workstreams

1. **Value Pool 0 — supplier readiness (primary research lane).** Study terrestrial industrial, workforce, infrastructure, acquisition, and documentation capacity needed to participate in complex supply chains. Research novelty is **not established**; literature review precedes instrument finalization and field studies.
2. **New Orleans East / Lake Forest demonstration (partner-led concept).** Explore place-based readiness and an optional place-making layer for the Michoud Innovation Corridor. The former Lake Forest Plaza vicinity is a **candidate study geography**, not a controlled or approved project.
3. **New Orleans Futurism (concept / expert-review lane).** Explore future New Orleans housing, streets, courtyards, water-aware infrastructure, and cultural continuity through performance-led design. **Not adopted City policy or a certified architectural standard.**

ORB's six-pillar policy framework encompasses development authority, land access, capital, infrastructure, local ownership and capacity, and protection and accountability.
