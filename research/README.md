# Research

**Primary research lane: Value Pool 0 / supplier readiness.** The subject is the capacity of small manufacturers, suppliers and procurement systems to turn technical capabilities and external obligations into controlled evidence of readiness.

The dedicated independent repository remains the system of record for study design and limitations: **[jvholmes87/orb-readiness-research](https://github.com/jvholmes87/orb-readiness-research)**. Do not copy its issue history, private correspondence, raw study evidence or research documents here without source review.

## Current stage

- Requirements gathering and focused literature review.
- No ORB field-study sample, participant dataset, partner commitment, qualification authority or demonstrated causal impact.
- No doctoral novelty claim until the literature-gap review and method validation support it.

A related but separate workstream, [Federal Submittal Compliance Model](https://github.com/jvholmes87/federal-submittal-compliance-model), examines the requirements/evidence mechanism in federal construction; do not treat its findings as transferable proof for ORB.
