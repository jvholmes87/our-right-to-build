# Evidence, claims and privacy policy

Aligned to the ORB folder README and source register, as working controls pending formal adoption.

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Primary official record, dated and identifiable |
| B | Official secondary source, such as an agency webpage or council presentation |
| C | Industry / consultancy estimate |
| D | ORB working concept, model or hypothesis |
| X | Unverified assertion — **never publish as fact** |

Public claims require a traceable source ID, date, context and scope. Visuals must carry a fact/assumption/concept label; inferred values must not be passed off as measured outcomes.

**Current boundaries:** No land control, legal status, City selection, official certification authority, guaranteed grants, suppliers, jobs, or development outcomes. A City-reported corridor pipeline is not delivered investment or employment. No participant dataset exists yet.

Keep an explicit **Not verified — do not cite** section for contested or unverified claims. Route material corrections through the [ORB Change and Progress Register](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fsWe2buAgQtcD2d-XKzdXNRufc5JL-ocWCpcoBoj8gs/edit); correct public assertions promptly under ORB's documented correction policy.

Exclude private correspondence, employer/contract data, identifiable supplier assessment material, nonpublic procurement information, sensitive participant information, personal identifiers and unlicensed imagery from public outputs.
