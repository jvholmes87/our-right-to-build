Our Right to Build / proposed initiative

Our land. Our communities. Our right to build.

Public promises mean little if communities and firms lack the land, infrastructure, capital, technical systems, and institutional capacity to turn opportunity into durable assets.

Our Right to Build (ORB) is a proposed Black-American reparative-development initiative examining the practical capacity to build. It is not an incorporated nonprofit or approved development program.

Explore the workstreams

Three workstreams

Research first. Development claims only when verified.

01 / RESEARCH

Value Pool 0

Supplier readiness, industrial capacity, workforce, documentation and acquisition constraints. Current stage: literature review and research design, not field-study results.

Review the research repository
02 / PLACE

New Orleans East

A potential readiness-assessment and place-making study around Lake Forest and the Michoud corridor. No site control or City appointment is claimed.

03 / DESIGN

New Orleans Futurism

Proposed design principles connecting local housing and public-space traditions to measurable comfort, resilience, access, and build capacity. Concepts require expert review.

Evidence and accountability

Make the capacity gap visible. Test what closes it.

ORB distinguishes primary official records, secondary government reporting, industry estimates, working hypotheses, and unverified assertions. Illustrations are not permits, forecasts are not guarantees, and assessment is not certification.
