## ORB-specific evaluation queue

1. **V009** — inventory current images/models/source and license provenance.
2. **V001** — source-linked supplier-readiness process diagram.
3. **V002** — Value Pool 0 layered architecture figure.
4. **V003–V005** — corridor evidence infographic, ORB explainer, synthetic-data readiness dashboard.
5. **V006–V008** — Future Shotgun, Lake Forest Commons, and lunar capacity explainers after supporting reviews.
6. **V010** — compare quality, speed, accessibility, mobile weight, and reproducibility of candidate stacks.

See [Visual Upgrade TODO](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fsWe2buAgQtcD2d-XKzdXNRufc5JL-ocWCpcoBoj8gs/edit) for acceptance criteria, evidence gates and owner decisions.

## Adoption criteria

License and version verification; live maintenance/security review; asset rights; accessible transcript/alt text; responsive performance; measurable improvement against an existing still diagram; reproducible source; clear distinction between verified conditions, simulations, assumptions and concepts.

No packages are installed in this initial setup.
