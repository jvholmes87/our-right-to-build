# Contributing to ORB

**Status:** This repository is for a proposed research and development initiative. Contributions do not imply institutional partnership, acceptance of a policy proposal, or endorsement.

1. Open an issue describing the problem, intended audience, source materials, and measurable acceptance criteria before substantial implementation.
2. Use a feature branch and a focused PR against `main`; do not push directly to `main`.
3. Identify external claims with source ID, publication date, evidence grade (A/B/C/D/X), and limitations. Unverified (X) claims must not appear as facts.
4. For any maps, imagery, footage or quantitative graphic, document rights, provenance, assumptions and the visual's **verified / model / illustrative / conceptual** status.
5. Keep operational, participant, company, installation, contract, personnel, confidential and security-relevant information out of the public repo. Seek explicit permission before transferring private datasets or employer materials.
6. Propose tests, include accessibility/mobile checks, and preserve public-facing disclaimers.
7. Check [CONTENT_LICENSE.md](CONTENT_LICENSE.md) before adding or modifying a non-code document or visual. Do not assume repository presence means that assets are under CC BY 4.0; record original authorship/rights, included third-party elements, and any separate permissions.
8. No merge, release, domain changes or deployment until the owner approves the specific gate in [release controls](docs/RELEASE_GATES.md).

This project reserves the right to reject work inconsistent with its evidence, community accountability, or legal safeguards.
