# Brand and asset management

**Status: placeholder structure only.** No final ORB visual identity or commercial media rights are represented as approved by this repository.

Proposed organization: `brand/source/` for editable originals, `brand/export/` for reviewed web-safe assets, and an asset manifest recording owner, author, provenance, license, dimensions, alt text, version, and approval.

Only add media that ORB has permission to reuse. Do not upload font files, stock imagery without a suitable license, agency seals, City logos suggesting affiliation, or unapproved conceptual site drawings.

Brand message from the founding framework: **Our land. Our communities. Our right to build.** Major external brand/identity changes require owner sign-off.
